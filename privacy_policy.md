RealtyWorks provides a business-to-business property-management work interface
for landlords and property managers. This Privacy Policy explains how we

## 1. Scope and our role

This policy applies when you:

- visit a RealtyWorks website;
- hold or use a landlord, property-manager, staff, or vendor account;
- receive and use an authorized vendor access link;
- create, view, update, or administer a RealtyWorks workspace;
- upload work-order photos, receipts, invoices, or other documents;
- contact us for support or otherwise communicate with us; or
- receive an operational email, SMS message, or push notification from us, if
  those channels are enabled.

### RealtyWorks as a controller or business

Depending on the law that applies, RealtyWorks acts as a "controller" or
"business" for Personal Information we determine how and why to process. This
generally includes website, account-administration, support, security,
diagnostic, privacy-request, and service-communication information.

### RealtyWorks as a processor or service provider

Our business customers determine why and how their workspace data is used.
When RealtyWorks processes that data to provide the Service, RealtyWorks acts
as the customer's "processor," "service provider," or "contractor," as those
terms are defined by applicable privacy law.

Workspace data may concern customer personnel, vendors, tenants, residents,
property owners, or other people. The customer is responsible for:

- having authority to collect and place that information in RealtyWorks;
- providing any notices and obtaining any permissions or consents the law
  requires;
- configuring user access appropriately;
- avoiding unnecessary or excessive Personal Information; and
- responding to requests concerning data the customer controls.

If your Personal Information appears in a RealtyWorks workspace maintained by
one of our customers, direct your request to that customer first. We will
assist the customer as required by our agreement and applicable law. We may
also tell you which customer controls the information when we can lawfully and
reasonably identify that customer.

This policy does not govern a customer's independent privacy practices or a
third-party website, application, or service that links to or from RealtyWorks.

### Customer Content can contain additional information

Work orders, notes, support messages, and uploaded files are unstructured. They
may contain additional Personal Information that we do not request and cannot
predict. Customers and users should submit only information that is relevant,
necessary, and lawful for the maintenance or property-management purpose.

Do not upload Social Security numbers, driver's-license or passport numbers,
payment-card data, bank credentials, medical records, or other highly sensitive
information unless RealtyWorks has expressly added a feature designed and
approved to handle that information.

### Photos and metadata

An uploaded photo or document may reveal people, property interiors, unit
conditions, receipts, addresses, or other private details. Files may also carry
metadata, including capture time, device details, or location information. We
do not plan to request precise device geolocation as a standalone feature, but
we may process metadata that remains embedded in a file a user uploads. Users
should remove unnecessary metadata and avoid including bystanders or unrelated
private information.

### Information we do not plan to collect or infer

RealtyWorks does not plan to:

- collect biometric identifiers or use facial recognition;
- collect precise device geolocation as a standalone feature;
- build advertising profiles;
- infer sensitive characteristics about individuals;
- obtain Personal Information from data brokers;
- use customer content to train generalized artificial-intelligence models; or
- use automated decisionmaking to make significant decisions about housing,
  employment, independent-contractor opportunities, credit, lending,
  healthcare, or education.

If these practices change, we will conduct the required review, update this
policy, and provide any notice or choice required before the new processing
begins.

We will not use Customer Content for advertising, sell it, share it for
cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to train a generalized AI

## 8. Cookies, local storage, service workers, and privacy signals

### Essential storage

RealtyWorks uses or plans to use cookies and similar browser storage that are
necessary to:

- authenticate users and maintain secure sessions;
- remember security and privacy choices;
- prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate requests;
- balance requests and deliver the Service; and
- preserve limited application state needed for expected functionality.

These technologies are operational, not advertising technologies. Blocking
them may prevent sign-in or other essential features from working.

### PWA and service-worker storage

If the installable PWA is enabled, a service worker may cache application-shell
files needed to load and update the installed experience. Offline mutation of
sensitive work-order data is not part of the current plan. We will document any
future offline Customer Content storage before enabling it.

### Push subscriptions

If web push is enabled, your browser or device creates a push subscription and
shares its endpoint and related public encryption keys. Push is optional. You
can revoke permission in your browser or device settings and, where available,
in RealtyWorks notification preferences.

### Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers provide a Do Not Track ("DNT") setting. There is no single,
generally accepted standard governing DNT responses. Because RealtyWorks does
not plan to use cross-site advertising trackers, a DNT signal does not change
the essential operational processing described above.

Where applicable law requires it, we recognize legally valid opt-out preference
signals, including Global Privacy Control ("GPC"), as a request to opt out of
sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. RealtyWorks does not currently engage
in those activities, so there is no sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising
processing for a GPC signal to disable. Essential authentication, security, and
service processing continues because it is not an advertising sale or share.

## 11. Security

RealtyWorks uses or, before accepting the relevant production data, will use
administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the
nature of the information. Planned safeguards include:

- encrypted network connections using current transport security;
- encryption at rest where supported by the selected infrastructure;
- authenticated accounts and revocable vendor access;
- role-, organization-, property-, and assignment-based access controls;
- PostgreSQL Row Level Security and database constraints;
- server-side validation and permission checks for privileged actions;
- audit trails for important changes and notifications;
- limited personnel and provider access based on business need;
- secret management and separation of production from development data;
- dependency, vulnerability, static-analysis, and secret scanning;
- logging, monitoring, backups, restoration testing, and incident response; and
- periodic review of providers, access, and retention.

No security program can guarantee that information will never be lost,
misused, or accessed without authorization. Users must protect their devices,
sessions, and access links. Vendor links may be forwarded, phones may be
shared, and screenshots may be copied. Do not place gate codes, lockbox
combinations, unnecessary tenant contact information, or other high-risk
details in a link-reachable vendor view.

If a security incident affects Personal Information, we will investigate,
contain, and provide notices required by applicable law and our customer
agreements.

## 13. SMS, email, and push communications

### Operational communications

Depending on the features enabled, we may send account notices, authentication
links or codes, vendor assignments, work-order status alerts, delivery
confirmations, security notices, or scheduled operational digests. These are
service communications, not marketing messages.

Before sending automated SMS messages, RealtyWorks will record the recipient's
appropriate consent or other lawful basis, the source and time of that consent,
the sending number, the relevant preference, and any revocation. A customer or
administrator must not submit another person's phone number for messaging
without authority to do so.

Message and data rates may apply. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or
undelivered messages.

### SMS choices

Recipients can use recognized opt-out words such as **STOP**, **CANCEL**,
**END**, **QUIT**, **REVOKE**, **OPT OUT**, or **UNSUBSCRIBE**, or use another
reasonable method we make available. We will process valid revocations and may
send one final message confirming the opt-out. **HELP** may provide assistance
where supported.

Opting out of SMS does not withdraw unrelated privacy choices or prevent
communications the law permits or requires through another channel. However,
it may prevent access to a feature that necessarily depends on SMS. When
reasonably available, the customer administrator may provide an alternative
authorized access method.

Marketing SMS is not currently planned. If RealtyWorks later sends marketing
messages, we will obtain any separate consent required and will not treat
agreement to this Privacy Policy as marketing consent.

### Push choices

Push notifications are optional and require browser or device permission. You
can disable them in browser or operating-system settings. Uninstalling a PWA or
clearing browser data may also invalidate a subscription, but you should use
the available permission or preference controls when you want to stop push
notifications.

## 14. Your privacy choices and rights

RealtyWorks offers the following baseline rights to people in the United States,
even when a particular state privacy law does not require every right. These
rights remain subject to identity verification, customer control of workspace
data, and legally permitted exceptions.

You may request to:

- **access or know** the Personal Information we maintain about you and how we
  use and disclose it;
- **correct** inaccurate Personal Information;
- **delete** Personal Information, subject to applicable exceptions;
- **obtain a portable copy** of Personal Information you provided, where
  technically feasible;
- **withdraw consent** when processing relies on consent, without affecting
  processing already lawfully completed;
- **manage communications** and notification preferences;
- **opt out** of sale, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling, although
  RealtyWorks does not currently engage in those practices; and
- **appeal** a denial when applicable law provides an appeal right.

We will not discriminate or retaliate against you for exercising a privacy
right.

### How to submit a request

Submit a request using either of these methods:

- Email: [PRIVACY EMAIL — REQUIRED]
- Privacy request form: [PRIVACY REQUEST FORM URL — ADD WHEN AVAILABLE]

Describe the right you want to exercise and the account, workspace, email

We will verify a request using information reasonably related to the account

### Authorized agents

An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf. We may require proof
that you gave the agent signed permission and may contact you directly to
verify your identity and authorization. A valid power of attorney may satisfy
the authorization requirement where applicable law provides.

### Timing and appeals

We will acknowledge and respond within the period required by applicable law.
For the nationwide baseline, we generally aim to provide a substantive response
within 45 days. If reasonably necessary and legally permitted, we may extend

If we deny a request, we will explain the basis and provide appeal instructions
